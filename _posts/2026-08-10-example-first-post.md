Posts are plain Markdown files in the `_posts/` folder. The file name must
start with the date: `2026-08-10-example-first-post.md`. Everything between
the `---` lines at the top is the post's settings — title, a one-line
description, and tags.

## A heading looks like this

Regular paragraphs need no markup at all. You can make text **bold** or
*italic*, add [links](https://gaofei9.github.io), and quote things:

> A quotation stands out like this — useful for a line from a paper or a
> point you want the reader to remember.

- Lists work too
- Just start lines with a dash

Each tag on a post becomes its own page automatically — click a tag above
the title to see it.
